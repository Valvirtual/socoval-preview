What we do

Complete services
for your pool

From installation to daily maintenance — we cover everything so you don't have to worry about a thing.

Regular maintenance

Periodic inspection of your pool — filters, pump, pH and chlorine — to keep it in perfect condition at all times.

Pool installation

Full design, excavation and construction of custom pools for private clients, communities and sports facilities.

Professional cleaning

Deep cleaning of the pool shell, walls, floor and equipment. We remove algae, limescale and accumulated dirt with specialist tools.

Repairs

Diagnosis and repair of faults — leaks, pump issues, cracks, lighting and any technical problem.

Water treatment

Full chemical analysis and balancing — pH, chlorine, alkalinity and hardness. Crystal-clear, safe water all year round.

Season opening

Complete preparation of your pool for the swimming season — full equipment check, filling and water adjustment.
